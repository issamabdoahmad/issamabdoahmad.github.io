Teaching

Teaching economics

I teach university economics one on one, mostly introductory microeconomics and macroeconomics. I have more than seven years of private tutoring experience, and my students have come from universities and colleges across Canada and the United States, including Concordia, McGill, the University of Toronto, Queen's, Dalhousie, Boston University and UCLA. Since 2022 I have ranked first among more than 500 Canadian economics tutors on Superprof, based on consistent five star reviews.

What I teach

Introductory microeconomics

Supply and demand, consumer and firm choices, market structures and welfare.

Introductory macroeconomics

GDP, inflation, unemployment, fiscal and monetary policy, and growth.

Economic development

Growth, poverty, institutions and policy in developing economies.

Labour economics

Labour supply and demand, wages, human capital and labour market policy, with Canadian examples.

Finance and econometrics

Financial models, regression and statistical inference in Stata, Python and Excel.

How it works

Format

Online by video call, with an interactive iPad whiteboard and complete notes after every session. In person sessions in Montréal are also possible, depending on availability.

Who it is for

College and university students who want clear explanations and steady progress, from a first economics course to more advanced topics.

Rates

$50 CAD per hour online. In person sessions in Montréal cost extra, depending on availability, so please ask when you book. Start with a 15 minute consultation to talk through your course and goals, and we will then arrange your sessions.
