Research

My research applies econometrics and machine learning to questions in health economics, firm performance and labour markets, using large national and international datasets.

Research interests

  • Regional economic disparities
  • Community economic development
  • Entrepreneurship and small business
  • Policy and program evaluation
  • Child health and health policy
  • Applied econometrics
  • Machine learning methods in applied economics
  • Firm performance and MENA economics

Current work

Labour market analysis in Québec

At CEDEC, I analyze regional labour markets and community outcomes using Statistics Canada, ISQ, OECD and Services Québec data, with a focus on English speaking communities.

Regional indicators

I build and maintain regional indicator databases on demographics, labour markets and community outcomes that feed briefs and economic profiles.
