Hiking

Group hike guidelines

Please read this before you reserve a spot on a MTLRoadTrips group hike. By reserving, you confirm that you have read it and accept it.

Reservation and payment

A spot is reserved once your Interac e-Transfer is received. Payment is required to reserve. You will know the total before the hike day.

Cancellation

Cancel at least 2 days (48 hours) before the hike for a full refund. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before the hike are not refunded. If I cancel the hike because of weather or safety, everyone is refunded in full.

Participation at your own risk

Hiking involves real risks, including uneven terrain, changing weather, wildlife and injury. By joining, you confirm that you understand these risks and that you take part at your own risk. I organize the outing and share the driving, but I am not a certified guide.

Health and fitness

Please join only if you are in good health and fit enough for the distance, elevation and difficulty listed for each hike. If you are unsure, ask me before you book. I am happy to assist.

What to bring

Required on every hike: water and electrolytes, snacks, proper hiking footwear, layers for changing weather, a waterproof jacket, a small backpack, a charged phone, a power bank, and a charging cable.

Recommended, and needed on some hikes: a cap, sunglasses, sunscreen, mosquito repellent, a headlamp for longer hikes, and a swimsuit with a towel and dry clothes when we expect a swimming spot. The details of each hike will say what applies.

On the day

Please arrive on time at the meeting point and stay with the group. Delays of more than 5 to 10 minutes are not tolerated, and the group may leave without you. I may change the route or turn back if conditions become unsafe.

Car rental

The cost of the car rental is split equally among everyone in the car, at exactly what it costs.

Photos

Scenery photos may be shared online. When you reserve, I will ask whether you agree to appear in group photos shared on the MTLRoadTrips pages and this website. You can say no, and you can ask me at any time to remove a photo of you. Group photos include only people who said yes.
